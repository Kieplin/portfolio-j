I worked as an assistant to the lecturer at [AMU](https://amu.edu.pl/) Cybersecurity Summer School 2025 while I was a graduate student at [WMI](https://wmi.amu.edu.pl/). I helped professors deliver classes on password security, login methods, cookies, and browser security. We used YubiKeys to generate OTPs for 2FA and for FIDO2 passwordless sign-in. To explore an example WordPress cookie exploit, we used VirtualBox VMs.
