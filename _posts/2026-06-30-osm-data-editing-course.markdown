While studying Geographic Information Science, I completed a short course in OpenStreetMap, instructed by Włodzimierz Bartczak, vice president of OpenStreetMapPoland ([@Cristoffs](https://www.openstreetmap.org/user/Cristoffs)). I learned the methods of mapping according to the guidelines and realized the significance of OSM data, while contributing to the tasks from [MapRoulette](https://maproulette.org/) and [UN Mappers and Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team](https://tasks.hotosm.org/).
